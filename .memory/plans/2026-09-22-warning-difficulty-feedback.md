# Plan: Warning Difficulty and Feedback

## Goal

Center in-game card panels and refine the WARNING/BOSS rhythm so it is less predictable, more demanding at special walls, and visually clearer. The measurable result is: panels are centred in the viewport; WARNING/BOSS entry text appears once for one second; every completed segment schedules the next at a random 10--15 score gain; both special timing windows are halved; and a successful special dash visibly punches through the wall while passed walls remain visible until they leave the screen.
